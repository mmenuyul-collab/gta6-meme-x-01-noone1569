# How this meme was made

The final `meme.png` was generated with an AI image model, inside the same
working session that maintains this repository, so the picture and the repo
share one continuous history. No part of the final image was drawn with code,
and no earlier draft pixels survive in it.

## The prompt

One generation prompt carried the whole composition — scene, mascot, and
captions together:

> A vibrant square meme illustration in bold GTA 6 art style. a cute white
> ghost mascot with sparkly star-shaped eyes, holding a magenta-pink round
> coin with a white star, huge and in the centre of the scene, lounging back
> on the hood of a sleek sports car at a Vice City beach sunset, wearing black
> sunglasses, one arm resting on a big money bag with a huge $ sign, paper
> banknotes floating in the air around it, totally relaxed and rich.
> Background: pink and purple sunset sky, orange sun, palm tree silhouettes,
> turquoise sea, neon-lit Leonida skyline, clean composition with a few large
> elements. Bold white meme caption text with thick black outline at the top:
> 'WE GOT AI AGENTS CASHING BOUNTIES' and at the bottom: 'BEFORE GTA 6'. The
> caption spelling must be exactly as written. Square 1:1 image, high
> contrast, the ghost and the captions readable even when the image is small.

## Generations

One generation was produced, and it was kept. The first render already landed
everything the entry needed: the white make.fun ghost — sparkly star eyes,
magenta star coin in hand — sits huge and central on the sports car's hood,
sunglasses on, leaning against a money bag, with the Leonida sunset, palms,
sea and neon skyline behind it, and both captions spelled exactly as briefed.

## Checks before keeping it

- Full-size view: ghost unmistakably the star of the scene; composition clean;
  caption spelling verified word by word.
- A 480x480 downscale was rendered and viewed: at that size the ghost, the
  coin, the money bag and both caption lines are still clearly readable.
- Final file: `meme.png`, 1600x1600 PNG.

An earlier hand-coded version of this entry's meme existed and was scrapped
by the user as too crude; its script and image were deleted, and this
AI-generated picture replaces them completely.
